# PotPress user requirements

What filter factories, fabrication shops, QC technicians and labs need, written from their side. They are drawn from the users and stakeholders in PPR-PRB-001 and are not yet validated with a filter factory; co-design sessions will revise them. Product requirements that meet these needs are in PPR-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix PPR-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Standard filter | Form the factory's standard flowerpot filter, about 10 L working volume | High | Small factory (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Even walls | Walls even enough, pot to pot, that fired filters flow inside the factory's band | High | Small factory; QC technician |
| UR-003 | Performance | Daily output | Press 50 or more filters a day with two workers | High | Small factory |
| UR-004 | Deployment and assembly | Local build and repair | Build and repair the frame with a stick welder, grinder, drill press and hand tools | High | Fabrication shop; NGO |
| UR-005 | Deployment and assembly | Local molds | Have the molds cast locally from open files | Medium | Fabrication shop |
| UR-006 | Logistics | Two-person handling | Move and assemble every part with two people and simple lifting gear | Medium | Fabrication shop; small factory |
| UR-007 | Safety | No reach into the press | Operators cannot reach a pinch point while force is applied | High | Small factory; safety authority (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-008 | Usability | Same flow test every time | Test every fired filter for flow and record it the same way each time | High | QC technician |
| UR-009 | Cost | Affordable | Cost well below an imported press, within a start-up budget | High | NGO; small factory |
| UR-010 | Environment and durability | Fits the workshop | Fit an existing workshop floor without mains power | Low | Small factory |
| UR-011 | Safety | Safe for drinking water | Nothing in the press or test rig contaminates the clay or the test water | High | Households; NGO (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-012 | Performance | Repeatable settings | Know and repeat the pressing setting between batches | Low | University or research lab |
| UR-013 | Maintenance | Local spares | Replace wear parts from regional markets | Medium | Small factory |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.

UR-013 (local spares) is met by PR-013, added on 2026-10-24 and decided by Amish on 2026-10-25 (D8).
