# PotPress STEM check

**Verdict: open (one row to confirm).** PotPress's working principle, a hand-pumped hydraulic press forming clay between cast molds, sits within known science, and 36 of its 37 claims are within known science (29 for option A, eight for options B and C added on 2026-10-24 from their detailed kit 1.9 models). One row is open: the independent review of 2026-10-24 showed that the pump handle force at the 6.4 t working force limit (S8) had no margin once the jack's pump and seal efficiency is counted. Amish decided D9 on 2026-10-25 ("i concur with your recommendations"): the working force limit is now 5.4 t, where the handle force is about 148 N with 85 % efficiency, and 10 t is a structural check case only. S8 stays **Plausible, to confirm** until the EN 1005-3 assessment with the bought jack's data is done. `project.yaml` records `stem_check: open` until it closes. The first check (v0.1) found four details that failed with a fix available: clay shrinkage, heavy lifts, pump handle force and the scrap alloy. Under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 ("whenever a STEM check fails propose and implement the best fix to the design and constructability"), each fix is now in the model, drawings, calculations, BOM and requirements (PPR-DDR-006). An independent reviewer then tried to refute the fixes and two were corrected, as described below. Method: STANDARDS section 20, fix rule. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, results in `docs/04-calcs/stem_results.csv`.

## The four fixes (PPR-DDR-006)

1. **Clay shrinkage (M6, D2).** The reference filter (280 mm inner rim, 240 mm deep, 15 mm wall) is now the fired size, and the mold cavities are that size divided by (1 - 0.08) for an assumed 8 % total linear shrinkage. The cavities are 304 mm at the rim and 261 mm deep, with a 16.3 mm wall. The 8 % is the middle of the 6 to 10 % that earthenware filter mixes show. It is one parameter (`CLAY_SHRINK` in `cad/src/model.py`), to be replaced by a test bar on the partner's production mix fired in its own kiln (ASTM C326 method) before any pattern is printed. A 2 % error moves the fired rim by about 6 mm. Knock-on changes: mold flanges 450 to 480 mm, flash groove radius 195 mm, dowels at 216 mm, frame 22 mm taller (overall 1,828 mm). The charge is now 9.6 kg, against the 8 to 9.5 kg factories report, and the fired pot is 4.9 kg. The QC rack holds fired pots and is unchanged.
2. **Heavy lifts (B2, D3).** Every part over 25 kg goes on a 0.5 t chain block (BOM item 22). The female mold lifts by two M12 DIN 580 eye bolts in its steel base plate, and the male mold by two in its flange top, each on a two-leg sling with shackles. The beams and platen go in a round sling. Eye bolts come out and the holes are plugged before pressing.
3. **Pump handle force (S8, D4).** The working force was limited to 6.4 t, where the frictionless handle force reaches the 150 N screening limit (PR-004). With 85 % pump and seal efficiency counted, the limit is 5.4 t (D9, decided by Amish on 2026-10-25). If pressing trials need more, a separate two-speed hand pump at waist height goes to Amish as decision D6.
4. **Scrap alloy (C3, D5).** The molds use A356 cast alloy wheel scrap only, sorted before melting to remove lead balance weights, brass valve stems, steel and paint. A chill sample of each melt is checked by spark OES, or by handheld XRF for lead, before pouring. Lead and cadmium must be within the EN 601 food-contact limits, recorded per heat (PR-012). The limit values are still to confirm.

## Corrections made to earlier numbers

- **Fired pot mass (C1).** Rice husk ash (about 18 % of the husk) is now in PPR-CAL-001; with the larger pressed pot the fired pot is 4.9 kg.
- **Cast aluminium yield.** Lowered from 90 to 80 MPa, as-cast remelted A356 without heat treatment. The male flange works at 60 MPa at 1.5 times the jack rating.
- **Joint bolts (S3).** Checked against the bolt shear resistance of 400 MPa (EN 1993-1-8).
- **Tipping (M5).** Restoring moment 850 N m with the heavier molds and the pressed pot taken at 8.72 kg (independent review, 2026-10-24).
- **Printer (E1).** Each pattern quadrant is now 243 mm on a 250 mm bed; with the 2 degree flange draft about 5 mm is left, so a true 250 x 250 mm bed is required.
- **Guards (E3).** ISO 13857 Table 7 (lower limbs) and the floor gap are still outside the desk check.

## Independent review of the fixes

A second agent, given the model, the calculations, the STEM script and the BOM but not the reasoning, tried to refute each fix (2026-10-23).

- **Shrinkage:** held. Uniform scaling is right to first order, the casting shrink still applies on top, travel (91 of 110 mm) and opening (291 of 310 mm) still work. It noted that the larger pot needs about 11.8 t for the mean pressure of the earlier design (now 0.57 MPa at the 6.4 t limit), that the charge is just above the factory range, and that the BOM text was stale. All three are now recorded or fixed.
- **Lifting:** weak as first drawn. The female eye holes sat in the lapped stop face, a fitted eye bolt would be crushed under the male flange, and the eye collar overhung the flange edge. Corrected: female lift points moved to the steel base plate, male holes to 214 mm, a two-leg sling with shackles, and holes plugged before pressing.
- **Handle force:** refuted as first drawn, with a 400 mm slip-on extension. A sleeve must overlap the handle, the extended tip reached about 61 mm off the floor and crossed the QC rack, and jack makers prohibit extensions. Corrected: no extension; the working force is limited to 6.4 t, and a remote two-speed pump is proposed to Amish (D6).
- **Scrap alloy:** weak. A melt test alone catches contamination too late, XRF misses magnesium and reads silicon poorly, and LM6 has little strength margin. Corrected: scrap sorted first, OES preferred, A356 only, yield taken as 80 MPa.

## Fields not relevant

None. Every field applies: physics (structure, hydraulics, statics), chemistry (clay, firing, alloys, silver), biology (ergonomics, dust and fume, the filter's microbial role), mathematics (geometry, rates, balances) and engineering (printing, finishing, guarding, lifting).

## Concept options

- **Option A, hydraulic bottle-jack press:** this check.
- **Option B, toggle lever press:** within known science (rows OB1 to OB3, checked on the detailed model). With the 1.1 m lever rigid on the upper link and 300 N at its grip, the toggle gives F = T / (2 L sin theta): about 0.16 t at 30 mm from straight, 1.4 t at 0.35 mm and 7 t at 0.014 mm. The force at the stop is therefore set by the clay charge, not by the operator (PPR-OPT-001). The 70 x 40 mm lower link and the 30 mm clevis pins carry 1.5 times 10 t with a wide margin.
- **Option C, power-screw press:** within known science (rows OC1 to OC3). A Tr40 x 7 screw with a thread friction coefficient of 0.15 and the detailed model's thrust ball bearing gives about 6.7 t for two people pulling 300 N at a 450 mm capstan radius (about 3.6 t with a plain collar). The screw self-locks (lead angle 3.5 degrees against a friction angle of 8.8 degrees); about 43 turns cover a 300 mm opening (PPR-OPT-001).
